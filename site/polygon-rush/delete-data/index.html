Delete your Polygon Rush data

Polygon Rush · Soft-Game Brothers

Polygon Rush has no accounts and no sign-in. Most of your data lives only on your phone. If you use friends or Community levels, our game server also keeps a little game data. Here's how to delete all of it.

Delete what's on our server (in the game)

  1. Open Polygon Rush.
  2. Go to Friends.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete friends data, then confirm.

This deletes right away, for good:

  • your racer name and #number, and the random player key
  • your friends list, friend requests, blocks and invites
  • your profile card and skin as stored on our server
  • every level you shared to Community, with its plays, thumbs up and reports
  • the record of Community levels you played, rated or reported

Can't open the game? Ask us by email

Email privacy@softgamebros.com with the subject "Delete my Polygon Rush data" and your racer name with its #number (shown at the top of the Friends screen, for example Neon Fox#0427). We delete the same data listed above within 30 days and reply when it's done. Parents can ask for their child's data the same way.

Delete what's on your phone

  • Settings > Reset Progress clears your progress, gems, skins and settings.
  • Create > My Levels lets you delete levels one by one.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the phone, including My Music songs (which are never uploaded).

What we keep, and for how long

  • Nothing from the list above is kept after you delete it.
  • If you stop playing, your server data is deleted automatically after 2 years without being seen.
  • If another player reported a public build room you were in, our safety team may keep that report (a snapshot of the level) for up to 1 year to keep players safe.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play, and ad data by Google. Manage those in your Google account and device settings.

More detail is in the Polygon Rush privacy policy.
