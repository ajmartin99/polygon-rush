Polygon Rush

Rush through endless, colourful polygon worlds. Tap, hold and flip as you shape-shift between four polygons, race friends online and build your own levels.

What's inside

  • Four shapes, one buttonJumper, Glider, Roller and Hopper each play differently.
  • Endless levelsSix worlds that speed up as you go. Every level has its own song, and the course is built to its beat.
  • Race and duelCPU rivals, two players on one phone, or online rooms with a code.
  • FriendsAdd friends by Name#number, see who's online and invite them to race.
  • CreateBuild levels, share a code, or build together with up to 4 friends.
  • Made for familiesGenerated names for under-13s, preset Quick Chat, parental controls and G-rated ads.
