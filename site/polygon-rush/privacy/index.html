Polygon Rush privacy policy

Soft-Game Brothers · Effective 14 October 2026

The short version

Polygon Rush has no sign-in. Players under 13 always get a generated racer name; players 13 and older may type their own racer and level names, which are filtered. Your progress and the levels you build are saved on your device. With the game server, your racer name gets a #number and you can add friends. There are no accounts and no typed chat: players talk only with Quick Chat’s preset phrases and emotes. Players under 13 never get public rooms. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, the birth year you entered on first launch, your racer name, the levels you build or import and any songs you add to My Music are stored on your device. Songs in My Music are never uploaded or shared. Uninstalling the app deletes them all. A backup code (Settings > Move to a new phone) contains this progress so you can move it yourself; we never see it.

Age screen

On first launch we ask for a birth year. It is used on the device to set up ads, parental controls and which online features are offered (Quick Chat for everyone; public rooms and typed names only from 13). It is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. All ads are limited to the "G" (general audiences) content rating. For players under 13, ad requests are tagged as child-directed and are non-personalised. Players 13+ in regions that require it are asked for consent through Google’s consent form, which can be changed any time in Settings > Privacy Choices. AdMob may process device identifiers and IP address as described in Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Game updates

When the app starts, it checks GitHub (where our game files are hosted) for a newer version of the game and downloads only the files that changed. These requests carry no account or personal information; like any download, GitHub sees the device's IP address as described in GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. A grown-up check is shown before any purchase for players who are not adults, and parents can turn purchases off in Settings > Parental Controls.

Online rooms (Play with Friends, Build Together)

Online rooms connect players directly (peer-to-peer) using a short room code; a signalling server only helps them find each other. Players in a room share their racer name, skin, game profile card (level, badges, title, game stats, any profile add-ons such as a featured Community level and, for players 13+, their #number so others can send a friend request), race positions, level edits and Quick Chat choices (preset phrases, never typed text). Like any internet connection, players in a room can see each other’s IP address. Parents can turn online play and Quick Chat off in Settings > Parental Controls.

Friends and your #number

When the game server is available, your racer name, a random #number and a random key created on your device (stored by us only as a one-way hash) are sent to our server. This lets friends add you by Name#number, which is never searchable or listed. We store your racer name and #number, whether you are under 13 (so typed names stay off for you), your friends list, friend requests, blocks, when you were last online and what you are doing in the game (menu, playing, building or in an online room), invites, which hold only a room code, and your latest game profile card and skin (game facts only, the same card players see in a room). Friends can see whether you are online, open your profile card and get invites from you. Players who open one of your shared Community levels can open your profile card too (without your #number if you are under 13). Nobody else can. There are no messages. Under-13s can only add a friend by typing their exact Name#number, shared in person. Parents can turn friends off in Settings > Parental Controls. Invites are deleted after 10 minutes; players not seen for 2 years are deleted with their friends lists.

Level codes

Sharing a level creates a code containing only the level itself, its name and the racer names of the players who built it.

Community levels

When you share a level to Community, our server stores its level code (the level, its name and the racer names of its builders) linked to your player, plus how many players played, beat and liked it. Anyone using Community can play it, sees your racer name without your #number and can open your game profile card. We also store which community levels you played, beat, liked or reported, so counts stay fair and Suggested shows levels you haven’t played. There is no text besides level names, which for players 13+ pass the same filter as racer names. Players can report a level; several reports hide it until our safety team looks. You can unshare a level any time in Create > Community > My Shared. Parents can turn Community levels off in Settings > Parental Controls.

Public rooms and Quick Match (13+)

A host aged 13+ can list a race or build room publicly, and Quick Match joins one at random. The list shows the room code, a generated room name and colours, the host’s racer name and the number of players. Race rooms leave the list when the race starts. If someone reports a public build room, a snapshot of the level is sent to our safety team.

Beta tester sign-up (website)

If you sign up to test Polygon Rush on our website, we store the email address you give us (the Google account used on the phone), and, if you add them, your first name, phone model and a short note, plus how you'd like to test. We use them only to invite you to the test, send you the download and ask for feedback. Sign-ups are for adults (18+, or a parent or guardian signing up for their child). We keep them until the test ends or you ask us to delete them at privacy@softgamebros.com.

Analytics

Polygon Rush does not include any analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children

Polygon Rush is designed to be suitable for all ages and follows the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: they keep generated racer names, add friends only by a typed Name#number and cannot use public rooms. There is no typed chat for anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset Progress or uninstall the app to delete data on your device (delete levels in Create > My Levels). Delete your #number, friends list, invites and shared levels from our server in Friends > Delete friends data. Ad-related data held by Google can be managed in your device’s Google settings.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@softgamebros.com. Safety concerns: safety@softgamebros.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
