# Google Play listing — Polygon Rush

## App name (≤30)
Polygon Rush

## Short description (≤80)
Shape-shift through endless polygon worlds! Jump, flip, glide & race friends.

## Full description (≤4000)
Rush through endless, colourful polygon worlds in a one-button runner made for everyone!

Tap, hold and time your moves as you SHAPE-SHIFT between four polygons:
■ JUMPER: tap to jump over spikes and bounce up stairs
▲ GLIDER: hold to fly through gates and tunnels
⬢ ROLLER: tap to flip gravity and roll on the ceiling
◆ HOPPER: tap to hop over spike floors

FEATURES
• Endless Rush: infinite levels that are never the same twice, getting faster as you go
• Race mode: dash against 3 CPU rivals, grab ? boxes for boosts, shields and snowballs, and climb from Bronze to Polygon Legend
• 2-Player Duel: split-screen racing on one device. Perfect for siblings and friends
• Play with Friends: private online races with a 4-letter room code (no chat, no strangers)
• Six worlds: Bubblegum Bay, Mint Meadow, Sunset Circuit, Ocean Prism, Candy Cosmos and Lemon Lab, each with its own catchy original soundtrack
• 23 skins with silly faces (sunglasses, kitty, dino, robot…) and 11 sparkly trails
• Daily gifts, missions, levels and rewards
• Kid Mode: slower speed and extra hearts for younger players
• Parental controls, no free-text chat, and kid-safe ads

Easy to learn, hard to master. How far can YOU rush?

## Data safety form
* **Data collected:** Device or other IDs (advertising ID) — collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads/analytics/fraud prevention, **not** for users under 13 (child-directed requests). Optional: approximate location inferred from IP address by Google ads.
* **Data shared:** with Google (AdMob) for advertising.
* **Encrypted in transit:** yes. **Users can request deletion:** yes (Reset progress / uninstall; game data is local only).
* **Purchase history:** handled by Google Play Billing (no payment info reaches the app).
* **Online play:** racer name (generated), skin and race positions go peer-to-peer to players in your private room only. Not stored.
* No account, no analytics SDK, no personal info, no contacts, no location permission.
